📖 How to Use This Tool

  1. Check the email you received for the password reset link
  2. The link should look like: https://...#access_token=...&refresh_token=...
  3. If clicking the link shows "unable to open", copy the tokens manually:
    • Find access_token= in the URL and copy everything after it until the next &
    • Find refresh_token= and copy everything after it until the next &
  4. Paste the tokens into the form above and click "Set Session"
  5. Once the session is set, you can reset your password
